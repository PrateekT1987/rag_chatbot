Demo storefront

This page shows the catalog chatbot widget embedded in a normal website. Click the round button in the corner to open the chat, then ask about prices, specifications, availability or comparisons between products.

Accurate answers

Responses are generated only from the product CSV — never invented.

Product cards

Matched products appear as cards with SKU, price and availability.

Mobile friendly

On phones the chat opens full-screen with safe-area support.

Embedding it on your own site

Add a single script tag wherever you want the chat widget to appear:

<script src="http://YOUR-SERVER:8000/widget.js" data-title="Store Assistant" async></script>

Options: data-api-url, data-title, data-subtitle, data-color, data-position="left|right", data-suggestions="Q1;Q2".
